## In my own words (the recall rep)
- A binary place-value ladder **always bottoms out at 1 (2⁰)**, never at 2. The rightmost
  bit is the 1s place — it's literally the "is this number odd?" bit. My first-attempt bug
  was starting the ladder at 2¹, which doubled every answer.
- **n bits all set to 1 = 2ⁿ − 1.** Reason: add 1 to all-ones and it rolls over to the next
  power of 2 (`11111 + 1 = 100000`), so all-ones is one less than that power. This is why
  8-bit maxes at 255, 16-bit at 65535, 32-bit at ~4.3 billion.
- Decimal → binary method: subtract the largest power of 2 that fits, mark a 1, repeat.
