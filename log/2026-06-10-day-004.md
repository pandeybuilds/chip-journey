## In my own words (the recall rep)
- **The three fundamentals are NOT/AND/OR**; XOR/NAND/NOR build from them. AND = "both",
  OR = "either", XOR = "differ". Truth tables are finite so every law is *verifiable*, not
  trusted.
- **De Morgan's, the lifelong version:** "To beat an AND, beat EITHER; to beat an OR, beat
  BOTH." Mechanically: **break the bar, flip the operator (AND↔OR), and negate EACH piece.**
  Memory anchor = the bouncer ("need list AND ID → rejected if not-list OR no-ID") and the
  laptop ("buy if cheap AND fast → won't buy if not-cheap OR not-fast"). The "negate each
  piece" step is the one that's easy to drop — check it consciously.
- **NAND universality:** every logic function can be built from NAND alone.
  NOT = NAND(A,A); AND = NOT(NAND(A,B)); OR = NAND(NAND(A,A), NAND(B,B)) — and the OR
  construction is literally De Morgan's law in disguise. This is the foundation Nand2Tetris
  (L2) is built on.
